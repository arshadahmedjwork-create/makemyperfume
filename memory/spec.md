# Make My Perfume — Testing Facts

- No login is required.
- Seeded products: Citrus Noir, Amber Veil, Nocturne, Azure Skin, Velvet Fig, Saffron Neroli.
- Checkout demo accepts any valid Indian guest details; prepaid confirms a mocked order, COD creates a pending delivery order.
- Product and news data can be replaced in `backend/routers/catalog.py` and `backend/routers/content.py`.
- Payment and email integrations are MOCKED boundaries, not live provider calls.
